# CycloMeasure — overview

**Live address:** https://cyclomeasure.space
**Who can use it:** Cyclo staff with an @cyclogroup.com.au email address.
**Owner:** Jordan Hyslop.

## What it is
CycloMeasure is Cyclo's own web app for marking up and measuring construction plans: architectural, structural and fit-out drawings. It opens in a web browser, so there's nothing to install. It's built to be simpler and quicker than Adobe or Bluebeam for everyday plan work. It complements those tools rather than replacing them.

## What it does
- **Opens plan sets:** one multi-page PDF or several PDFs at once, with a thumbnail sheet list.
- **Reads the drawings:** picks up sheet numbers, titles and drawing scales automatically from the title block. This works on scanned or "flattened" council DA plans too, using OCR (reading text from the image).
- **Teach the title block:** if a consultant's title block isn't recognised, box the sheet number, title and scale on one sheet, and it reads the same spot on every sheet.
- **Scale:** detected scales are shown as "Detected — please verify". Calibrate from any known dimension line. Sheets printed at a different size from the scale's stated sheet (e.g. "1:100 @ A1" on an A3 page) are flagged as resized and must be calibrated.
- **Takeoff (measuring):**
  - Linear (lengths), Area (with cut-outs), Perimeter and Count.
  - Draw by clicking corners, dragging a rectangle, or freehand.
  - Quantities are grouped into items (e.g. "Floor tiles"), with totals per sheet and for the whole project.
- **Markups:** revision clouds, rectangles, ellipses, arrows, lines, pen, text boxes and highlights, in a choice of colours and line weights.
- **Export marked-up PDFs:** this page, only the pages with markups, or the whole set. You can include item names and quantities on the drawings. The original drawings stay sharp.
- **Projects:** saved automatically as you work. You can share a project as a single file that includes the PDFs.
- **Focus mode and full screen,** so the plan fills the screen in meetings.

## How it works (and why it's private)
- The app is a website, but **plans are never uploaded**. All processing happens on your own computer, inside the browser. Client drawings don't leave your machine.
- The site is locked to Cyclo staff. You sign in with your Cyclo email and a one-time code.
- Your work is saved in your browser, on that computer. It isn't automatically on other computers or other browsers. Use Export / Import to move or share it.

## Good uses
- Quick quantities: floor areas, wall lengths, counts of fixtures.
- Marking up plans for clients, consultants or site.
- Client meetings: open the plans, mark them up live, and export a marked-up copy straight after.
- Checking DA plan sets downloaded from council portals.

## Limits to know
- Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. It isn't designed for phones.
- Measurements depend on a correct scale. Always verify a detected scale or calibrate before relying on quantities.
- Text in exported PDFs uses a standard font. Unusual symbols (like ≥ or ✓) appear as "?".
- Revision clouds are rectangular.
- There's no CSV or Excel quantity export yet.
- Clearing your browser's history or site data for this site deletes saved projects. Export important projects as a backup.
