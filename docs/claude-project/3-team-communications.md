# CycloMeasure — team communications

## Launch email (sent by Jordan)

**Subject:** CycloMeasure: a simple new tool for marking up and measuring plans

Hi team,

Following the recent questions about Adobe and Bluebeam, I wanted to share a tool we've built for exactly this kind of work. It's called CycloMeasure.

It isn't meant to replace Adobe or Bluebeam. It's a simpler, faster option for everyday plan work, and it runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to install.

**What it does**
- **Mark up plans:** clouds, arrows, text, highlights, shapes and freehand.
- **Measure plans:** lengths, areas and counts, grouped into items such as "Floor tiles" or "Internal walls", with running totals.
- **Export marked-up PDFs:** a single page, only the pages with markups, or the whole set.
- **Read the drawings:** it picks up sheet numbers, titles and scales automatically, including on most scanned council plans.

**Good for client meetings**
Open the plans, mark them up as you talk, and export a marked-up copy to send straight after the meeting.

**Your plans stay private**
Plans are never uploaded. Everything happens on your own computer, inside the browser.

**How to sign in**
1. Go to https://cyclomeasure.space in Chrome or Edge. It's our own address.
2. Enter your @cyclogroup.com.au email address.
3. Check your inbox for a one-time code and enter it. You'll stay signed in for a week.

**Good to know**
- Your work saves automatically, in the browser on the computer you're using.
- To share a project or move it to another computer, use Project → Export project and send the file. The other person opens it with Project → Import project.
- It's designed for a laptop or desktop, not a phone.

It's new, so I'd love your feedback. If something doesn't work or you'd like a feature, let me know.

Thanks,
Jordan
